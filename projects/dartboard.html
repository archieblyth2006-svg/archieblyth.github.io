Dartboard — game-ready asset

A dartboard prop modelled from reference in Blender and textured in Substance 3D Painter with layered PBR materials at 2048×2048.

BlenderSubstance 3D Painter

How it works

Blocked out in Blender from a cylinder against real dartboard reference placed in the viewport. The wireframe shows the radial layout: 20 scoring wedges with double and triple rings cut into the mesh, and curved number text following the outer rim.

Two texture sets (metal rim, playing surface) at 2048×2048. Layered smart materials, brushed worn aluminium over steel rust, sharpening passes and fabric grain on the sisal face, give a worn pub-dartboard feel while staying efficient for real-time rendering.
